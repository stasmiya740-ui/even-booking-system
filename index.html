Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel or change my booking?

You can manage your booking by contacting our support team via email or phone up to 48 hours before the event.

Are the tickets refundable?

Refunds are available up to 72 hours before the event date. After that, tickets can only be exchanged.

Can I transfer my ticket to someone else?

Yes! Ticket transfers are free and can be done through your confirmation email link.

How do I receive my tickets?

Your tickets are sent instantly to the email you provided at the time of booking.

Do you offer group discounts?

Yes, groups of 10 or more receive a 15% discount. Contact us directly to arrange group bookings.
